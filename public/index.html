Browser support and privacy

Voice recognition depends on your browser and may send speech to the browser's speech service. Keep this page open and your screen awake for the best chance of hearing the wake phrase. A normal website cannot reliably listen while your phone is locked or the browser is in the background; that needs a native Android app with additional permissions. This prototype does not place phone calls.
